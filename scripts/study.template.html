Would you approve this change?

What you will do

  • You see 12 change plans, one at a time, each with the ticket it was written for. Some are good and some are not.
  • Each plan is shown in one of two formats, chosen at random: the raw plan with the cluster's facts, or a checked report. Read whatever helps you decide.
  • Choose Approve or Reject. A one-line reason is optional but helps. There is no going back.
  • It takes about 15 to 25 minutes. Answers are revealed at the end.

The page saves your decisions, the time each took and your reasons, under an anonymous id, for the project owner to read. No name or email is stored. You can stop and come back later on the same account.
